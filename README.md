KrishiDhan
AI-Powered Transparent Paddy Marketplace & National Agricultural Stock Intelligence System

Overview

KrishiDhan is a proposed digital agricultural platform designed to improve transparency in the paddy market and strengthen agricultural stock intelligence in Bangladesh.

The platform aims to connect farmers with government procurement channels and verified private buyers, allowing farmers to compare available offers and make informed selling decisions.

Beyond market connectivity, KrishiDhan aims to provide government authorities with aggregated agricultural production, procurement, transaction, and warehouse stock information to support better planning and decision-making.

Key Objectives

- Improve transparency in the paddy marketplace.
- Reduce dependency on unverified and opaque intermediaries.
- Connect farmers with government and verified private buyers.
- Provide fair-price insights to farmers.
- Maintain agricultural production and stock information.
- Support government procurement and food security planning.
- Explore AI-driven price forecasting and agricultural stock intelligence.

 Proposed Core Modules

1. Farmer Information Management
2. Paddy Marketplace & Buyer Verification
3. Government Procurement Integration
4. Warehouse & Stock Management
5. Fair Price Recommendation
6. Agricultural Data Analytics
7. AI-Based Price and Production Forecasting

Current Development Status

Phase: Concept Development & Research**

The project is currently in its planning and research stage. Requirements analysis, system design, technical research, and architecture planning are the initial priorities.

No functional implementation has been completed yet.

Long-Term Vision

To develop an integrated agricultural marketplace and intelligence platform that contributes to a more transparent, data-driven, and efficient agricultural ecosystem in Bangladesh.
